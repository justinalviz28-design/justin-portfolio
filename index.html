About

Started in graphics and social media. Grew into the systems behind them.

I got my start creating graphics, reels, and short-form content for podcasts and real estate brands. That work pulled me into GoHighLevel — building the funnels, forms, pipelines, and automations those campaigns actually ran on — and from there into AI: chatbots and conversational agents that handle lead capture, follow-ups, and support without a human watching every conversation.

I've worked as a third-party marketing partner across a range of client brands — realtors, a medical spa, and other businesses — covering everything from ad support to email marketing to the automation layer that ties it together. I'm also finishing a degree in Public Administration at Bulacan State University, which is where the process-and-systems thinking comes from.
